Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

1 Introduction

Rubik's Cube ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your information when you use our game.

2 Information We Collect

We collect very little information:

  • Global leaderboard: The leaderboard is provided through Google Play Games Services. If you sign in, your Google Play Games player name and your scores or solve times are processed by Google so they can be shown to other players. We do not receive your Google account email address or password.
  • Saved settings: If you use cloud saving, your game settings are stored in Google's cloud and are linked to your Google account, so they can be restored on your devices. We do not use them for anything other than running the game.
  • Contact form: If you use the contact page to report a bug, suggest a feature, or send us a message, we temporarily receive your name, email address, and the content of your message. After we respond, your name and email address are deleted immediately. We do not store or use them for any other purpose.

3 Camera and Local Network

  • Camera: The game asks for camera permission only when you choose to scan your cube. Camera images are used only to detect the colors of your cube. They are processed on your device and are never saved, uploaded, or shared.
  • Multiplayer: Two-player games are played over your Wi-Fi network. Game data is exchanged directly between the two players' devices and is not collected or stored by us.

4 Information We Do NOT Collect

Apart from what is described above, we do not collect, store, or share any personal data on our own servers. We do not run our own user accounts, and we do not collect your location, contacts, or photos. Data handled by Google Play Games Services and Google's cloud is managed by Google, as described in the next section.

5 Third-Party Services (Google)

This game uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect certain information automatically (such as device ID, IP address, and usage data) to serve relevant ads. AdMob is a third-party service governed by its own privacy policy.

This game also uses Google Play Games Services (for the leaderboard) and Google's cloud storage (for saved settings). These services are provided and operated by Google, and any data they handle is governed by Google's privacy policy.

Please note: Google AdMob, Google Play Games Services, and Google's cloud services are operated by Google as independent third parties. We encourage you to review their privacy policy at
https://policies.google.com/privacy

6 Your Choices

You can clear data stored on your device at any time by uninstalling the game or clearing app data in your device settings. You can turn off camera access at any time in your device settings. You can manage or delete your Google Play Games profile, leaderboard scores, and saved game data from the Google Play Games settings on your device or from your Google Account. If you need help with this, or would like your scores removed from the leaderboard, email us at the address below.

7 Children's Privacy

Our game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8 Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted here with an updated date.
